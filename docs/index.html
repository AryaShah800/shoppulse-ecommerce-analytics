ShopPulse: late deliveries, reviews and repeat buying

Olist Brazilian e-commerce, Jan 2017 to Aug 2018, about 99k orders. Money in Brazilian reais (R$), item price only.

62.4% vs 9.2%
Late orders get 1-2 star reviews at 6.7x the on-time rate (p < 0.001).

Action: Treat lateness as the main driver of review score; make on-time rate a headline KPI.

Impact: 6.7% of reviewed orders are late, yet they produce 33% of all 1-2 star reviews.

32% to 79%
The 1-2 star share climbs with every extra day late (1-3 d, 4-7 d, 8+ d: 32%, 68%, 79%).

Action: Send a proactive message or goodwill gesture once an order is 4+ days past its estimate, before the customer reviews.

Impact: Targets the 4+ day bucket (4,527 reviewed orders in the dose-response table) where reviews turn mostly negative. The gesture's effect is untested.

81.0% on time
On-time delivery fell to 81.0% in March 2018 (85.9% in Feb 2018, 87.6% in Nov 2017) against 93-99% in most months.

Action: Add delivery-estimate buffer and review carrier capacity before peak and carnival months.

Impact: These three months hold the largest on-time shortfall; a better estimate is cheaper than a bad review.

1.35% vs 1.87%
Late first deliveries repeat-buy less within 180 days, but the 0.52 pp gap is NOT significant (p = 0.053).

Action: Do not fund retention on delivery alone. Run a randomised goodwill test on late orders and measure 180-day repeat.

Impact: Upper-end value is small: R$1,658 (range R$0 to R$2,919) over 13.8 months. Observational, not proven causal.

58% of revenue
Two segments, 30% of customers, make 58% of item revenue; the lapsed one (R$3.9M) last ordered about 397 days ago.

Action: Run a win-back test aimed at lapsed high spenders; repeat buying is rare overall (3.04% of customers order twice).

Impact: R$3.9M of historic revenue sits in 13,998 lapsed high spenders. Response rate unknown, so test small first.

Read with care. Observational data. Late delivery is strongly linked to bad reviews, but its link to repeat purchase is borderline (p = 0.053) and the test could only detect gaps of about 0.68 pp or more. Every number comes from this repo's code (results/).
